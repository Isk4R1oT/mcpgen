## Background

Phase 9.1 introduces the anonymous hero flow: a user pastes an OpenAPI URL, generates an MCP server in 60s, and previews the result without signing up. Two abuse-prevention surfaces touch user data:

1. **Anonymous IP rate limiting** (D-02 + D-11) — 1 generation per IP per 24h, backed by a TimescaleDB hypertable storing `sha256(daily_salt + raw_ip)`. Per Article 29 Working Party guidance and GDPR Recital 26, salted+hashed IP is **pseudonymized** data (NOT anonymized) and remains in scope for GDPR.
2. **HttpOnly anonymous session cookie** (D-04 + D-19) — `mcpgen_anon_session=<ULID>; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Secure (in prod); Path=/; Max-Age=604800` — links a user's anonymous generations on their browser so post-signup the rows can be claimed.

The privacy policy MUST disclose both, the legal basis for processing, the retention boundaries, and the data-subject erasure path.

## Disclosure Language (drop-in for `apps/web/src/content/privacy.md`)

Use the following sections **verbatim** under a top-level heading such as "How we handle data for unauthenticated users".

### What we collect

For anonymous (unauthenticated) usage:
- **Hashed IP address** — `sha256(daily_salt + raw_ip)`. We never store your raw IP address.
- **Anonymous session cookie** — an opaque ULID (`mcpgen_anon_session`) set as `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` in production. The cookie expires 7 days after issuance.
- **Generation metadata** — the OpenAPI URL or spec content you submitted, the generation ID, and timestamps. Spec content itself is never logged outside the generation pipeline.

For signed-in usage:
- Standard authentication profile data managed by Logto (email, GitHub identity if you signed up via GitHub) — see Logto's privacy policy at https://logto.io/legal.

### Why we collect it

For anonymous IP hashing — **legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest)**. The legitimate interest is preventing abuse of our free anonymous tier (1 generation per IP per 24 hours; cost cap $0.50 per generation). We balanced this against your privacy by using pseudonymization (Article 4(5)) and a daily-rotating salt so the hash is computationally unrecoverable beyond the retention window.

For the anonymous session cookie — **legal basis: strictly necessary** (the cookie is required to authorize you to view your own anonymous generations across page reloads and to claim them when you sign up). No tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies are set on unauthenticated visitors.

### How we minimize what we collect

- **Raw IP address is NEVER stored.** Only the hash `sha256(daily_salt + raw_ip)` reaches the database. The raw IP exists only in the Cloudflare Worker memory for the duration of a single request.
- **Daily salt rotation.** Every day at 00:00 UTC the salt is rotated. The previous day's salt is preserved for 24 hours to bridge in-flight requests across the rotation boundary, then overwritten. After at most 48 hours from collection, the salt that produced any given hash no longer exists in our systems — the hash becomes computationally unrecoverable for practical purposes.
- **Cookie scope.** The anonymous session cookie carries no personal data — only an opaque ULID. It cannot be used to identify you across other sites; it has no third-party scope.
- **No tracking pixels, no analytics on unauthenticated traffic, no advertising cookies.**

### How long we keep it

| Data | Retention | Reason |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Raw IP address | Never stored | Data minimization (Article 5(1)(c)) |
| `daily_salt_current` and `daily_salt_previous` | 2 days maximum (rotated daily; previous-day salt overwritten on next rotation) | Bridge in-flight requests across rotation; minimal-necessary-for-purpose |
| Hashed IP in `anon_generation_log` (rate-limit ledger) | 30 days | Abuse forensics window; minimal-necessary-for-purpose |
| Anonymous session linkage in `anonymous_generations` | 7 days unclaimed | Allows claim of your generation after sign-up |
| Anonymous ephemeral deploy URL | 24 hours, then hard-deleted | Trial scope |
| Anonymous session cookie on your browser | 7 days (`Max-Age=604800`) or until you sign up (whichever first) | Matches the linkage retention above |
| Generation artifacts (the MCP server we generated for you) | Indefinite (no PII) | Reused across users via cache when the OpenAPI spec hash matches |

After 30 days, the salt that produced your hash has been overwritten and a single hash is computationally unrecoverable to any specific IP — the data is, in practice, anonymized. We retain it only in aggregate form for abuse-pattern analysis.

### Your rights

You have the right to request **erasure** of any pseudonymized data we hold about your anonymous usage (Article 17 GDPR). To exercise this right:

1. Email **privacy@mcpgen.app** with the IP address from which you accessed the service (this lets us recompute the hash on our side and find matching rows).
2. We will, within 30 days of receipt, delete:
   - Any matching rows in `anon_generation_log` from the most recent retention window.
   - Any matching rows in `anonymous_generations` from the past 7 days.
   - Any associated anonymous ephemeral deployments.

This process is currently manual; a self-service erasure interface is on our roadmap for a future release.

You also have the right to access, correct, restrict, or port your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR). For all such requests use the same email address.

### Cookies summary (unauthenticated visitors only)

| Cookie | Purpose | HttpOnly | SameSite | Secure | Lifetime |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|--------|----------|
| `mcpgen_anon_session` | Authorize you to access your own anonymous generations and claim them on sign-up | ✅ | Lax | ✅ (production) | 7 days from issue |

Signed-in users carry the Logto-managed session cookies in addition to (or instead of) this one. See Logto's privacy policy for those.

## GDPR Compliance Citations (for legal review record)

| GDPR Reference | Citation in policy |
|----------------|--------------------|
| **Article 4(5)** | Pseudonymization — salted+hashed IP is pseudonymized data per Article 29 Working Party guidance, NOT anonymized; remains personal data per Recital 26 and stays in scope for the duration of the retention window |
| **Article 5(1)(c)** | Data minimization — raw IP never stored; only hash + rotating salt; no tracking/analytics on unauthenticated visitors |
| **Article 6(1)(f)** | Lawful basis for IP-hash processing: legitimate interest (preventing abuse of free anonymous tier; cost-cap and rate-limit defense) |
| **Article 17** | Erasure honored via privacy@mcpgen.app — user supplies the IP they used; we recompute the hash and delete matching rows from the most recent retention window |
| **Article 33** | Breach disclosure: an `ip_hash` leak has materially lower severity than a raw-IP leak because the hash without the day's salt is not immediately re-identifiable |
| **Recital 26** | Pseudonymized data IS personal data and subject to GDPR — disclosure required |
| **Article 5(1)(b/e)** | Retention boundary cited as **minimal-necessary-for-purpose** — 30 days for the rate-limit log is the abuse-forensics window; daily-salt history is 2 days; anonymous session linkage is 7 days |

## Verbatim disclosure paragraph (RESEARCH §11)

For the policy section's lead paragraph, the following is reproduced **verbatim from RESEARCH §11** for traceability:

> **Anonymous abuse prevention.** When you use MCPGen without an account, we hash your IP address with a daily-rotating cryptographic salt and store the hash for 30 days. We never store your raw IP address. The hash lets us prevent abuse of our free tier (one anonymous generation per IP per 24 hours) without identifying you. After 30 days, the salt that produced your hash is deleted, making the data computationally unrecoverable. You can request earlier deletion by emailing privacy@mcpgen.app — provide the IP from which you accessed the service and we will delete matching hashes from the most recent retention window.

## Implementation evidence (Phase 9.1 plans)

So legal review can verify the schema matches the disclosure claims:

- **DB schema:** `infrastructure/neon/migrations/20260501010000_phase09_1_anon_flow.sql` (plan 09.1-04) creates `anon_generation_log` TimescaleDB hypertable + `app_secrets` salt store + `anonymous_generations` linkage table + `deployments.{anon_session_id, expires_at}` columns
- **Hashing primitive:** `apps/api/src/lib/anon-ip-hash.ts` (plan 09.1-05) — `sha256(daily_salt + raw_ip)`; raw IP exists only in Worker memory; never logged
- **Rate-limit middleware:** `apps/api/src/middleware/anon-rate-limit.ts` (plan 09.1-05) — dual-hash lookup with 24h grace window; localhost bypass for founder/CI; fail-open on DB outage
- **30-day retention cron:** `apps/api/src/inngest/functions/anon-rate-limit-cleanup.ts` (plan 09.1-10) — `drop_chunks('anon_generation_log', INTERVAL '30 days')` runs daily
- **Daily salt rotation:** `apps/api/src/inngest/functions/anon-salt-rotate.ts` (plan 09.1-10) runs daily at 00:00 UTC; `daily_salt_previous` preserved for the 24h grace window then overwritten
- **7-day anon-linkage cleanup:** `apps/api/src/inngest/functions/anon-generations-cleanup.ts` (plan 09.1-10) deletes unclaimed `anonymous_generations` rows after 7 days
- **24h ephemeral deploy expiry:** `apps/api/src/inngest/functions/anon-tenant-expiry.ts` (plan 09.1-10) hard-deletes anon CF Workers for Platforms tenants at `expires_at`
- **Sentry PII strip:** Phase 8 D-13 `beforeSend` already strips raw IPs from any error payload before they leave the runtime; reaffirmed in Phase 9.1 plan 10 audit (CTRL-08 + Pitfall #15)

## What plan 10-09 must NOT do

- Promise we delete data faster than 30 days for the rate-limit log — we cannot. The salt is gone after 2 days but the chunk physically remains in TimescaleDB until `drop_chunks` runs at the 30-day boundary.
- Use the term "anonymized" for the IP hash within the 30-day retention window — it is **pseudonymized** (Article 4(5)). These have different GDPR implications and a regulator will catch it.
- Reference Cloudflare Turnstile or any CAPTCHA — Turnstile is deferred per CONTEXT D-12 and would change the disclosure surface if added.
- Add tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies on unauthenticated visitors — none exist; the disclosure must reflect that.
- Drop the "anonymous session cookie" line — it is a real cookie set on every unauthenticated visitor and must be disclosed in any cookies table.
